# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People use Hermes while actively planning, deciding, and executing work across desktop chat and messaging surfaces. They need compact answers that help them understand what matters, choose a next action, and safely approve consequential changes without decoding implementation details.

## Product Purpose

Hermes is a persistent personal agent that learns from prior work, uses tools, and carries tasks across conversations and platforms. Success means the user can understand the current situation at a glance, trust what Hermes knows, and move forward with one clear action.

## Brand Personality

Capable, direct, and calm. Hermes should feel like a thoughtful operator: concise when the situation is simple, explicit when confidence or approval matters, and never theatrical.

## Anti-references

Dense debug dumps, spreadsheet-like cards that expose empty fields, tiny text, unexplained system vocabulary, decorative dashboard chrome, and interfaces that prove data was rendered without helping the user decide what to do.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the decision or next action, then make supporting detail available.
- Hide empty and low-value fields instead of filling the surface with unknown values.
- Preserve the user's language and reading direction, including reliable Hebrew RTL behavior.
- Keep powerful behavior flexible while making each visible interaction bounded and understandable.
- Require clear preview and approval before external changes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls and tables, visible keyboard focus, adequate contrast, screen-reader labels, and reduced-motion-safe behavior. Support mixed Hebrew and English content without broken ordering or ambiguous labels.
